plurimus

A Bevy-native terminal renderer, running in the browser: every frame is terminal cells, drawn to a WebGL2 canvas. Both demos want a keyboard. Source and the terminal versions are on GitHub.

Rat-Man

The 2d pipeline: a rat eats cheese through a maze while four birds hunt it, all halfblock pixel art. arrows or WASD steer, a power cheese turns the birds edible, r starts over, q comes back here.

Lander

The 3d pipeline: a GPU scene read back into cells, under a widget HUD. W or space burns, A/D tilt, t cycles the pixel-to-cell strategy, e the edge overlay, r resets, q comes back here. Needs hardware WebGPU; the first frames take a few seconds while pipelines compile.
